## 1. Purpose & Sovereign Objective

BACSWN is national infrastructure; it must ultimately be **operated and sustained by Bahamians**.
This plan builds that capability deliberately — a structured path from partner-led delivery to a
Bahamian-staffed watch office, NOC, and field-service organization. **Knowledge transfer is a
contracted deliverable, not a courtesy**, and reduces long-term dependence on the partner over the
Build-Own-Operate life of the system.

**Targets (illustrative, to confirm with the Ministry):** Bahamian staffing ≥ 60% by end of program
and ≥ 85% within three years of national acceptance, with all front-line watch and NOC roles
Bahamian-led.

## 2. Roles & Competency Framework

Training is **role-based**, each role mapped to a competency standard with assessed certification.

| Role | Core competencies |
|------|-------------------|
| **Meteorological watch officers** | Aviation weather, SIGMET/ICAO products, hurricane ops, COP/dashboard, decision-making |
| **NOC operators (T1)** | Monitoring, alert triage, runbooks, escalation, mesh/autonomy awareness |
| **Field technicians (T2)** | Node/radio install & repair, solar/power, antennas, IoT, lightning/grounding, safety |
| **Radar technicians** | Raytheon radar operation & maintenance (vendor-certified; ITAR-aware) |
| **Drone / UAS operators** | BCAA-licensed flight, survey/SAR/relay missions, airspace deconfliction |
| **Network & software engineers** | Mesh protocol, edge AI, hub, OTA, integrations |
| **Security / SecOps** | PKI/crypto operations, incident response, pen-test follow-up |
| **Emergency-coordination liaisons** | CAP/EDXL, COP, inter-agency procedures with NEMA/Police/Fire/RBDF |
| **Supervisors / managers** | Operations management, SLAs, governance, reporting |

---

## 3. Training Approach

- **Train-the-trainer:** build a core of Bahamian instructors early so capability is self-sustaining,
  not a one-off handover.
- **Blended delivery:** classroom + **hands-on at the pilot cluster** + e-learning + on-the-job
  apprenticeship/shadowing during commissioning.
- **Simulation-based:** use the platform's **simulation engine** to run weather, hurricane, and
  failure scenarios safely — including hub-loss/autonomous-mode and inter-agency activation drills.
- **Vendor-delivered specialist training:** Raytheon (radar), Iridium (satellite), NVIDIA/edge,
  scheduled around install milestones; vendor certificates recorded.
- **Competency assessment & certification:** written + practical; certification required before a
  trainee takes an unsupervised operational role; **recertification** on a defined cadence.

---

## 4. Phased Programme (maps to the Schedule)

| Phase | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **Foundational (M6–M10)** | Programme + curricula established; academic partnerships signed; first cohort + train-the-trainer begins; pilot-cluster hands-on |
| **Role-specific (M10–M18)** | Watch officers, NOC, field techs, radar, drone, security cohorts trained alongside rollout; apprenticeship during commissioning |
| **Operational readiness (M18–M23)** | Certification, hurricane-season and inter-agency drills, supervised live operations; Bahamian leads stood up |
| **Sustainment (M23+)** | Ongoing intake, refresher/recertification, continuous improvement, progression |

---

## 5. National Capacity Building & Partnerships

- **Academic & vocational partners:** University of The Bahamas, Bahamas Technical & Vocational
  Institute (BTVI), the Department of Meteorology, and BCAA — for curricula, accreditation, and a
  talent pipeline.
- **Pipeline & outreach:** internships, scholarships, and STEM outreach to build a durable local
  workforce for meteorology, networking, and aviation technology.
- **Localization roadmap:** progressive transfer of front-line, then supervisory, then engineering
  roles to Bahamian staff, tracked against the §1 targets.
- **Knowledge assets:** SOPs, runbooks (shared with O&M), maintained training materials, and a
  competency register owned nationally.

---

## 6. Governance & Records

- **Training records & competency matrix** maintained per individual and role; tied to operational
  rostering (O&M Plan §8) — only certified staff hold unsupervised duty.
- **Recertification cadence** and currency tracking (e.g., drone licences, security, watch
  qualifications).
- **Effectiveness review:** post-drill and post-incident learning feeds curriculum updates;
  annual review of localization progress with the Ministry.
