# AssetAdvisor Quick Start

Get from a new account to a working portfolio in about ten minutes.

AssetAdvisor tracks stocks, ETFs, futures, forex and crypto in one place. It shows live profit and loss, performance and allocation, scans the markets for unusual moves, and has an AI assistant that can explain what it sees. You can use it on your own, or with colleagues as a firm.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open https://assetadvisor.ai-elevate.ai and, under the sign-in form, choose **Create one**.
2. Enter your name, a username and a password of at least 10 characters.
3. If your company uses AssetAdvisor and you were sent an invitation link, open that link instead. It signs you up and adds you to the firm in one step.

If your organization has set up Google, Microsoft or company sign-in, use the matching button on the sign-in page.

## 2. Get your trades in

Use whichever of these suits you; you can mix them.

| Method | Where | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Connect a broker | Portfolio → **Broker Connections** | Alpaca accounts; trades and cash sync automatically every 6 hours |
| Import a file | Portfolio → **Import Trades (CSV)** | Exports from Binance, BTCC, Coinbase, Kraken or any broker |
| Type them in | Portfolio → **Trades** → Add Trade | A few positions, or anything not covered above |

For a CSV from an unlisted broker, choose **Other exchange or broker**. You'll see a preview, match the columns once, and AssetAdvisor remembers the mapping next time.

## 3. Look at your portfolio

- **Portfolio**: your positions, grouped by asset class, with live prices and unrealized and realized profit and loss.
- **Performance**: growth over time, monthly returns, and time-weighted and money-weighted return. Record deposits and withdrawals under **Cash movements** so the returns are accurate.
- **Allocation**: set target weights and AssetAdvisor lists the trades needed to get back to them.

## 4. Follow the markets

- **Dashboard**: indices, yields, commodities, crypto and your watchlist. Select **Customize dashboard** to reorder, resize or hide tiles and build your own Markets tiles. Use the search box at the top to open any instrument.
- **Charts**: every instrument page has a candlestick chart where you choose the range and the candle size (5 minutes to weekly) separately, with volume, RSI, Stochastic RSI and SurgeX underneath on the same time axis.
- **In-Play**: every 15 minutes the scanner flags instruments with unusual volume and a large, decisive candle, and finds the news behind the move. Turn on **Phone Alerts** (through the free ntfy app) to be notified on your phone.
- **Alerts**: get notified when a price, RSI or Stochastic RSI crosses a level you choose, either live or on a candle close (for example the 30-minute or 1-hour close).
- **WhatsApp**: link your number in the user menu to get alerts there and chat with the assistant from your phone.

## 5. Ask the assistant

Click the chat button at the bottom right of any page and ask in plain English, for example "How concentrated is my portfolio?" or "Log a buy of 10 AAPL at 190 yesterday". Before the assistant changes anything, it shows exactly what it will do and waits for you to confirm.

## 6. Optional: more than one portfolio

Under Portfolio → **Manage Portfolios** you can create separate portfolios (for example "Long-term" and "Trading"), and share one with someone as a viewer or manager. When you have more than one, a switcher appears in the top bar.

## 7. Optional: a firm account

If you manage money with colleagues, create a firm under Portfolio → **Firm Accounts**. You can invite members with a role (admin, portfolio manager, analyst, compliance or viewer), add firm portfolios, and set a sign-in policy. Firms are entirely optional; personal accounts have every portfolio feature.

## Where to get help

- Click **?** in the top bar for the interactive tour of the screens.
- **Help & Docs** in the user menu has the full User Guide and the FAQ, to read online or download as PDF.
